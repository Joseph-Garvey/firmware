What's modelled & what isn't
Faithful to firmware: ToA per preset, slot time, util-driven CW backoff, SNR-weighted rebroadcast delay & router offset, CAD defer (one slot for preamble detect), FLoRa-style preamble collision window, flood dedup + hop decrement, CLIENT dupe-cancel vs ROUTER, >40-node congestion scaling, TX queue cap (16) drops, and the SLM spectrum self-throttle (polite 25% ch-util + EU duty + 5% fair-share, per SoundLevelModule::dutyAllows). An SLM node emits the fixed-cadence spectrum; its Meshtastic role can be set to CLIENT (interval scales with congestion) or SENSOR (priority, no scaling).

Validated against meshtasticator: sensitivity, slot time, capture threshold, preamble collision window, and CAD slot offset all match lib/config.py and lib/phy.py. Our ToA uses the Semtech/RadioLib formula with coding-rate denominator directly; meshtasticator's airtime() adds 4 again to its config.cr, so its ToA values are ~1.4–1.8× higher for the same preset.

Path loss is parameterized differently here (range + exponent vs. the PTX/LPLD0/GAMMA/D0 model in meshtasticator) but both are log-distance.

Not modelled: DM unicast / NextHop routing, want_ack retransmits, MQTT uplink latency, mobility, inter-SF non-orthogonality.
